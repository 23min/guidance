# Writing prose

Rules for any prose you write for me: docs, comments, specs, commit messages
and replies.

- **Write for information.** Every clause carries something the reader needs.
  Cut what adds only emphasis or rhythm: a clause tacked onto a finished
  sentence (", and never times it"), a contrast with a claim nobody made
  ("this isn't a bug, it's a design choice") and a setup with a reveal
  ("There's one catch: timing."). Rhetoric calls the first epiphrasis; a
  journalist calls a punchy last line a kicker. A construction that carries a
  fact stays: a real contrast, or three items when there are three.
